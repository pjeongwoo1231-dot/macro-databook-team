ISSN 1020-0959 (print) ISSN 1682-7678 (online) Consumer preferences for a digital euro: insights from a discrete choice experiment in Austria Helmut Elsinger, Helmut Stix, Martin Summer* July 2025 This paper examines consumers’ intended adoption of a digital euro in Austria using a discrete choice experiment. We estimate a mixed logit model to quantify the role of key attributes such as privacy, offline functionality, security against financial loss, monetary incentives, and payment form fac- tors.
