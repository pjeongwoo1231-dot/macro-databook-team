## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

The …nancial channel of exchange rates operates through changes in risk-taking by investors and is re‡ected in the response of …nancial conditions to exchange rate movements. We show that stock returns also re‡ect the …nancial channel of exchange rates, with higher local currency stock returns associated with a weaker dollar. The broad dollar index emerges as a global factor, consistent with the …nancial channel operating through swings in risk-taking by global investors. We introduce the “dollar beta” as the sensitivity of stock returns to swings in the broad dollar index, and show that emerging market stock indices that have a higher dollar beta tend to have higher average returns, implying that the dollar beta is a cross-section risk factor that is priced.
