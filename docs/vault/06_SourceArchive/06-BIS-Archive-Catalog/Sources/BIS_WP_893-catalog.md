ISSN 1020-0959 (print) ISSN 1682-7678 (online) Assessing the fiscal implications of banking crises Claudio Borio, Juan Contreras and Fabrizio Zampolli • 12 October 2020 We propose a method for computing the distribution of the potential fiscal cost of a banking crisis – a key input in assessing the adequacy of a country’s fiscal buffers. First, we use a cross-section of banking crises to identify the risk factors that predict the post-crisis increase in public sector debt – a measure of the overall fiscal cost of a crisis. Next, we use these risk factors to compute country-specific distributions of that cost in the event of a crisis. We find that the level and growth of credit to the private non-financial sector, foreign exchange reserves and the ratio of bank capital to assets are relevant predictors.
