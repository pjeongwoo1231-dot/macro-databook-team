## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

Central banks can operate with negative equity, and many have done so in history without undermining trust in fiat money. However, there are limits. How nega- tive can central bank equity be before fiat money loses credibility? We address this question using a global game approach motivated by the fall of the Bank of Amsterdam (1609–1820). We solve for the unique break point where negative equity and asset illiquidity render fiat money worthless. We draw lessons on the role of fiscal support and central bank capital in sustaining trust in fiat money. Key words: central banks, negative equity, fiat money, trust
