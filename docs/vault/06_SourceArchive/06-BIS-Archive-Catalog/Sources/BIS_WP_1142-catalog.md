Parigi§ This version: November 2023 We analyse the impact of platform lending on innovation and e-commerce ven- dors’ surplus. The platform generates revenues from both lending and marketplace fees, and can use lending to price discriminate vendors, thereby leading to higher marketplace fees and below-market interest rates. Prohibition of platforms lending may stifle innovation, not because of lack of platform funding, but because the high fee policy of the platform deters banks from financing innovators.
