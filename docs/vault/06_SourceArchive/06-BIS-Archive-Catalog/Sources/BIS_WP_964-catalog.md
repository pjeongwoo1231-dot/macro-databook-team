## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

This paper presents a simple integrated macroeconomic model of a small, bank-dependent open economy with a managed float and financial frictions. The model is used to study the eﬀects of five types of policy instruments: fiscal policy, monetary policy, macroprudential regulation, foreign exchange intervention, and capital controls, in the form of a tax on bank foreign borrowing. It also consid- ers how, following a drop in the world interest rate, these instruments can be combined to restore the initial equilibrium. The analysis illustrates how macro- financial policies can complement each other in response to capital inflows. In particular, it is shown that, to stabilize the economy, whether monetary policy should be contractionary (a common prescription in practice) or expansionary depends on which other instruments are available to policymakers. The joint use of macroprudential regulation and capital controls is also shown to provide a potent combination to manage capital inflows.
