## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

We quantify the trade-offs of using reserve requirements (RR) as a financial stability tool. A tightening in RR reduces the amplitude of the credit cycle. This lowers the frequency and strength of financial stress episodes but at a cost of lower growth in credit and economic activity. We find that the gains from a lower probability and magnitude of financial stress episodes are greater than the costs from the initial reduction in economic activity. In addition, we find that RR have a stronger effect on emerging market economies than in advanced economies, both in terms of costs and benefits. Finally, we find that uniform RR have a stronger effect than RR that differentiate by maturity or currency.
