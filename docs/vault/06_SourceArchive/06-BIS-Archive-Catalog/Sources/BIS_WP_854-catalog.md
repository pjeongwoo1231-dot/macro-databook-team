## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

Designing operations for liquidity support requires central banks to properly measure and monitor bank funding risk in real time. We construct a new indicator of rollover risk for banks, called the forward funding spread. By accounting for market participants’ expectations of how funding costs will evolve over time, it serves as a better signal of the change in the stance of monetary policy than the usual spot InterBank Offered Rate–Overnight Interest Swap spread. Our indicator helps to contrast three liquidity regimes, which coincide with the levels of excess liquidity supplied by central banks. JEL: E32, E44, E52.
