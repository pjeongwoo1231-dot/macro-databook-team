## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

The macroeconomic effect of term premiums is a controversial issue both theoreti- cally and quantitatively. In this paper, we explore the possibility that term premiums affect inflation and the real economy via exchange rate dynamics. For this purpose, we construct a small open economy model with limited asset market participation, focusing on the empirical observation that uncovered interest parity holds better for longer-term interest rate differentials. A quantitative exercise using Japanese and U.S. data shows that changes in term premiums, particularly those made by the central bank’s bond purchases, have sizable effects on Japanese inflation rates via exchange rate dynamics.
