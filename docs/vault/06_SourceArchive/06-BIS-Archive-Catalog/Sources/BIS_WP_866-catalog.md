## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

We investigate empirically how the balance sheet characteristics of central counterparties (CCPs) affect their modelling of credit risk. CCPs set initial margin (IM), i.e., the collateral for trans- actions, to limit counterparty credit risk. When a CCP’s IM model fails on a large scale, the CCP could fail too, losing its skin-in-the-game capital.
