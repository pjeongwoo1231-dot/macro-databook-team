ISSN 1020-0959 (print) ISSN 1682-7678 (online) On Fintech and Financial Inclusion Thomas Philippon∗ September 2019 Abstract The cost of financial intermediation has declined in recent years thanks to technology and increased competition in some parts of the finance industry. I document this fact and I analyze two features of new financial technologies that have stirred controversy: returns to scale and the use of big data and machine learning. I argue that the nature of fixed versus variable costs in robo-advising is likely to democratize access to financial services.
