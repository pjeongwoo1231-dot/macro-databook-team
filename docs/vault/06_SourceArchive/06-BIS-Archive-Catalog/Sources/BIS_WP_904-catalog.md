## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

I establish that US public firms holding real estate have persistently lower levels of productivity than non-holders. Rising real estate values relax collat- eral constraints for companies that own real estate and allow them to expand production. Consequently, an increase in house prices reallocates capital and la- bor towards inefficient firms, with negative consequences for aggregate industry productivity. Industries with a stronger relative increase in real estate values see a significant decline in total factor productivity, and the within-industry covariance between firm size and productivity declines. My results suggest a novel channel through which real estate booms affect productivity and have implications for monetary policy.
