## 원문 초록 또는 원문 선두 발췌

This study investigates the eﬀects of the Bank of Japan’s (BOJ) exchange-traded fund (ETF) purchase program on stock returns, particularly focusing on the role of the stock lending market. Using ﬁrm-level panel data, we ﬁnd that the BOJ’s purchases raised stock returns more for those stocks with limited availability in the stock lending market. Nonetheless, over the longer term, the BOJ’s accumulated purchases lowered lending fees and weakened the eﬀects of their purchases on stock returns. This result suggests that ETF managers supply stocks that constitute ETFs held by the BOJ to the stock lending market, which weakens the policy eﬀects of the program.
